### How AI is changing research

### Cross-discipline sparks

- **[Mount Toba eruption doesn't seem like it could nearly kill our species](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/08/mount-toba-eruption-doesnt-seem-like-it-could-nearly-kill-our-species/)** (Ars Technica Science, 2026-08-09) — New analysis suggests the ~74 ka Toba super-eruption had surprisingly muted global climate impact, undermining the "volcanic winter" hypothesis for a human population bottleneck. For earth-system modelers and paleohydrologists, this reopens the question of how volcanic forcing propagates through the water cycle: if Toba's radiative forcing was insufficient to collapse regional monsoons, what *is* the threshold? Paleo-ESM runs (e.g. LGM, mid-Holocene) that include volcanic aerosol forcing could be re-examined with updated Toba estimates to better constrain the hydrology–volcanism link in Quaternary reconstructions.
