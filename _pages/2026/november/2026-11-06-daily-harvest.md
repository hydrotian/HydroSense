## Top-Tier Journal Papers

### Tree Rings Indicate Potential for Severe Soil Moisture Drought in California Is Much Greater Than Observations Suggest

**Authors**: Jacob Jones, A. Park Williams

**Journal**: *Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl125616](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125616)

**Matched topics**: drought, climate change
{: .label .label-green }

> Tree rings reveal that 1579–1580 was by far the driest pair of years across California since at least CE 1100. Here we produce a gridded tree‐ring reconstruction of summer soil‐moisture anomalies for California and perform a peaks‐over‐threshold extreme value analysis on statewide mean drought severity from CE 1100–2025. Results indicate marginal annual exceedance probabilities (AEP) of ∼0.24% for 1579 and ∼0.04% for 1580, with a two‐year block EP of ∼0.05% for 1579–1580. Considering only the instrumental record (1901–2025), AEPs for the drought severities reconstructed for 1579 and 1580 cannot be robustly estimated and, for 1580, the reconstructed severity appears essentially impossible. Our findings demonstrate that including tree‐ring reconstructions meaningfully changes the expected frequency of droughts on realistic planning horizons, such as a 1% AEP (100‐year) event. Modern risk assessments based solely on observations likely underestimate the true probability of extreme drought events, even without accounting for anthropogenic climate change.

### Enhanced Surface Coupling but Suppressed Convective Triggering During Humid–Hot Extremes in the Yangtze River Basin, a Major Humid‐Heat Hotspot in China

**Authors**: Jiangfeng Wei, Guojie Wang

**Journal**: *Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl125735](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125735)

**Matched topics**: river
{: .label .label-green }

> Compound humid‐hot extremes bring growing threats to human society, yet land–atmosphere feedbacks regulating these events remain insufficiently understood. Using high‐resolution observational and reanalysis data, we reveal that the Yangtze River Basin (YRB) is a prominent hotspot for humid‐hot extremes, with rare hot‐only events. Comparing humid‐hot and non‐humid‐hot days, we find that humid‐hot extremes are sustained by positive feedbacks involving a high‐pressure anomaly, suppressed clouds and precipitation, drier soils, enhanced evapotranspiration, and lower relative humidity—mutually reinforcing the extreme state. Land–atmosphere coupling strengthens substantially for near‐surface variables, indicating stronger land surface control. Yet this enhanced coupling does not extend to cloud development: elevated lifting condensation levels and reduced relative humidity inhibit cloud formation, leading to a decoupling between convection and cloud development and allowing humid‐hot extremes to persist. Our findings highlight the role of land surface processes in modulating compound extremes and provide a foundation for improving their prediction.

### High‐Resolution Discharge Inversion in a Small Alpine Watershed: Combining Monitoring Images and Triaxial Seismic Data

**Authors**: Shuang Liu, Kaiheng Hu, Pu Li, Hao Li

**Journal**: *Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl124730](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl124730)

**Matched topics**: runoff, flood
{: .label .label-green }

> In small alpine watersheds, abrupt rainfall‐ or meltwater‐driven runoff leads to dramatic variations in flow width over uneven channel beds, thereby complicating discharge monitoring. Using a typical watershed near the Yarlung Tsangpo Grand Canyon as the study area, we presented a workflow combining imagery and seismic data to estimate the instantaneous discharge with machine learning. Results show that image‐based flow width correction effectively improved discharge records to provide a better basis for modeling. For hourly instantaneous discharge estimation, the machine learning model achieved good performance, with mean coefficients of determination of 0.77 for the training, 0.68 for the validation, and 0.66 for the independent testing, and corresponding mean Kling–Gupta efficiency values of 0.83, 0.79, and 0.75, respectively. Using minute‐level seismic data, we further detected 179 suspected flood events between July and October 2025 and analyzed their hydrological characteristics. In general, this proposed framework could address the limitations of traditional monitoring approaches and support water resources management in similar small watersheds.
