## 今日亮点

一项针对南极西部锡普尔海岸的双向耦合建模研究表明，冰下水文与冰动力学之间存在深度相互作用：上游冰层变薄可形成水力势低点，促使水分汇聚并排入冰流，进而触发冰流B的脉冲式加速。重要的是，该研究发现将冰动力学驱动的可变融化率以及冰下湖几何形状纳入模型后，冰流运动反而趋于稳定——这一反直觉的反馈机制清楚地说明了为何单向（非耦合）模型会遗漏关键行为。对于致力于陆面与地球系统模型研究的水文学家而言，这项工作生动展示了为何双向耦合在冰冻圈模拟中至关重要。

## 顶级期刊论文

### Exploring Antarctic Feedback Mechanisms Using Two‐Way Coupled Subglacial Hydrology and Ice Flow Modeling in the Siple Coast

**作者**：Koi McArthur, Christine Dow, Shivani Ehrenfeucht

**期刊**：*Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2025gl121190](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025gl121190)

**匹配主题**: hydrology
{: .label .label-green }

> The Siple Coast region of West Antarctica is known for its fast flowing, topographically unconstrained ice streams that evolve due to subglacial conditions. In this work, we analyze the nature of the Siple Coast subglacial hydrologic system and its interactions with the ice sheet using two‐way coupled subglacial hydrology and ice flow modeling. Our modeling is consistent with observations of a water saturated till layer underlying the Siple Coast ice streams. We find that future thinning of ice upstream of Ice Stream B can lead to a hydraulic potential low that promotes water amalgamation and drainage through the ice stream, leading to pulses in ice motion. The inclusion of an ice‐dynamics driven variable melt rate and the alteration of glacier driving stress due to subglacial lake geometry lead to stabilized motion of Ice Stream B. These feedbacks demonstrate the importance of coupled modeling for projecting the evolution of ice dynamics.
