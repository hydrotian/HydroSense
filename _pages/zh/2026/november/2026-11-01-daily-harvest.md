## 今日亮点

一项基于欧洲高分辨率水文模型的新研究揭示，河网系统性地放大了干旱范围：积雪融水主导的上游地区径流亏缺会传播并在下游形成更大的径流干旱，而湖泊和水库则通过解耦局地产流与出口流量起到缓冲作用。这些发现对于预判大陆尺度干旱的时空演变具有重要意义，同时也强调了在大尺度水文模型中纳入河网拓扑结构和水体蓄水量的必要性。

## 顶级期刊论文

### How the River Network Shapes Spatiotemporal Drought Behavior Across Europe

**作者**: Joren Janzing, Niko Wanders, Steye L. Verhoeve‐Dwars, Manuela I. Brunner

**期刊**: *Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl122313](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl122313)

**匹配主题**: river, runoff, streamflow, reservoir, drought
{: .label .label-green }

> Understanding spatial drought relationships is important for effective drought relief. The river network—that is, the system of rivers, channels, lakes, and reservoirs within a river basin—influences spatial streamflow drought connectivity and modulates drought characteristics. However, these effects are difficult to disentangle from spatial drought dependencies originating from other hydro‐meteorological processes. Here, we use high‐resolution hydrological model simulations over Europe and study the river network effect in isolation by comparing drought extents of unrouted runoff to those of routed discharge. We find that the river network increases drought extent in most European basins. Runoff droughts in snowmelt‐dominated upstream regions often lead to larger discharge droughts than the original runoff drought, whereas discharge droughts in downstream rivers are affected by runoff droughts upstream. Water bodies influence the river network effect by storing water, thereby decoupling runoff from discharge. Our results can help water managers to better understand spatiotemporal drought development.
