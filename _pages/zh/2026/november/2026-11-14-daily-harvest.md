### Graph‐Based Automated Discovery of Concise Soil Hydraulic Functions From Data: Beyond Empirical Formulations

**作者**：Hao Xu, Jinshen Sun, Yuntian Chen 等

**期刊**：*Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl126105](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl126105)

**匹配主题**: hydraulic, water flow, vadose zone, soil hydraulic, water retention, hydraulic conductivity
{: .label .label-green }

> Soil hydraulic functions underpin water flow and transport modeling in the vadose zone, yet they are commonly represented by expert‐designed formulations such as the Mualem–van Genuchten (MvG) model. Because these formulations rest on predefined functional assumptions, balancing predictive accuracy and parametric compactness across soil textures remains challenging. Here we introduce a graph‐based equation discovery framework that distills soil hydraulic functions from experimental data. Reapplied to the original data sets that motivated the MvG parameterization, the framework recovers a concise water retention function together with an analytical unsaturated hydraulic conductivity expression whose mathematical structure departs from classical empirical forms. Evaluated across soils of contrasting texture, the discovered model predicts unsaturated hydraulic conductivity more accurately than MvG and its common variants, while its fitted parameters retain interpretable correlations with measurable soil physical properties. These findings suggest that data‐driven discovery can yield compact, analytically tractable constitutive relations that move beyond fixed empirical formulations.

### Estimates of Rare Storm Surges in Poorly Gauged Coastal Areas

**作者**：Pietro Devò, Thomas Wahl, Marco Marani

**期刊**：*Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl125482](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125482)

**匹配主题**: flood, coastal, storm surge
{: .label .label-green }

> Estimating low‐probability storm surges is essential for flood risk management, yet conventional asymptotic extreme value methods use only a small subset of observations and therefore produce large uncertainty at poorly gauged and ungauged sites. We present MEVD‐S, a new framework that combines the Metastatistical Extreme Value Distribution with an original regionalization scheme to overcome data scarcity in rare storm surge analysis. MEVD‐S uses all observed events to infer annual maxima and incorporates nearby observations through weights inversely related to site heterogeneity, avoiding rigid homogeneous regions and fixed spatial boundaries. This allows information to be integrated seamlessly across space and time while better representing the continuity of storm‐surge‐generating processes. Evaluation with tide‐gauge records from North America, Europe, and Japan shows that MEVD‐S outperforms at‐site Generalized Extreme Value (GEV) estimates and yields predictions at ungauged locations, with bias and uncertainty comparable to those obtainable from long local records.

### Low Frequency Icequakes Generated During Transient Cavity Drainage on a Greenlandic Outlet Glacier

**作者**：Hugo Rousseau, Florent Gimbert, Jules Le Bot 等

**期刊**：*Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl125244](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125244)

**匹配主题**: glacier, drainage
{: .label .label-green }

> Dewatering of weakly‐connected areas of glacier‐beds may drive long‐term ice deceleration in Greenland ablation zones. However, the underlying physical processes remain poorly understood. Here we constrain the physical processes involved in weakly‐connected cavity drainage using a dense seismic array and GNSSs. We report a swarm of seismic events at Isunnguata Sermia (Greenland), which we show to be Low Frequency Icequakes (LFIs). Using event detection from machine learning and localization from match field processing, we demonstrate that this swarm coincides with local ice surface subsidence and minimum pressure in the efficient drainage system. We argue these LFIs are generated by pressure diffusion from transient connections between weakly‐connected cavities dewatering into the efficient drainage system. We observe a swarm's migration associated with a diffusion coefficient of , likely driven by cavity connections through ice‐bed interface hydro‐fractures. This work provides the first observational constraints on transient cavity dynamics and hydrological model parameters.

### Spatial‐Temporal Compounding Summer Extremes Driven by Preferred Circulation Trajectories Over Europe

**作者**：Ruud Sperna Weiland, Karin van der Wiel, Frank Selten 等

**期刊**：*Geophysical Research Letters* · **DOI**: [10.1029/2026gl125220](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl125220)

**匹配主题**: precipitation, soil moisture, climate model
{: .label .label-green }

> Compound weather events can have severe societal and ecological impacts, yet their joint spatial–temporal dynamical drivers remain insufficiently understood. Here, we conceptualize spatial–temporal compound extremes as circulation‐driven extremes arising from a specific preferred circulation trajectory that can generate multiple extremes across space and time. Using a 2000‐year present‐day ensemble from the coupled climate model EC‐Earth, we map the statistical relationship between evolving jet stream patterns and temperature, precipitation, and daily soil moisture changes over Europe in summer. We show that preferred trajectories govern the spatial distribution, duration, and compounding of extremes. Prolonged or repeated extremes can also arise from successive trajectories with similar surface imprints, rather than from a single persistent circulation pattern. An illustrative analysis of two summers in ERA5 reanalysis shows that observed extremes can be interpreted as manifestations of preferred circulation trajectories. Our trajectory‐based perspective provides a framework for assessing spatial–temporal compound extreme risk over Europe.
